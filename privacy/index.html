This Privacy Policy describes how SHAWA GRILL HALAL LLC ("SHAWA," "we," "us," or "our"), a Florida limited liability company with its principal address at 101 SW 1st Street, PH06, Dania Beach, FL 33004, collects, uses, and protects information when you use the SHAWA GRILL ordering application and the website shawagrill.com (together, the "Service"). The Service lets guests view the menu of the SHAWA GRILL restaurant, place orders by scanning a QR code at a table, or order for pickup ("to-go") or delivery.

By using the Service, you agree to the practices described in this Policy.

1. Information we collect

1.1 Information you provide when ordering

  • Name — the name you enter for your order.
  • Phone number — used to identify your order, contact you about its status, and, for delivery orders, to reach you at delivery.
  • Order details — items ordered, quantities, modifiers and add-ons, table number (when ordering by table QR code), order type (dine-in, to-go, delivery), delivery address if you provide one, order notes, and the order total including tax.
  • Payment information — payments are processed by our payment provider (including Clover, where applicable). We do not store full payment card numbers on our systems; card data is handled by the payment processor under its own privacy policy.

1.2 Reviews and feedback

  • If you leave a review or feedback through the Service (including via a linked Google review page), we receive the content of that feedback and any name you attach to it. Promotional codes you redeem are recorded together with the order they are applied to.

1.3 Information stored on your device

  • The Service uses your browser's local storage (localStorage) and similar on-device storage to remember your cart, your table number, language preference (English / Russian / Spanish), and your current order state, so the Service works without requiring an account. This data stays on your device and is removed when you clear your browser data.
  • The Service does not require account registration and does not use advertising trackers.

1.4 Technical information

  • When you access shawagrill.com, the hosting and content-delivery providers may automatically log standard technical data such as IP address, browser type, device type, and pages requested, for security and operational purposes.

2. How we use information

We use the information described above only to:

  • prepare, fulfil, and deliver your order, and operate table service (including calling a waiter to your table);
  • contact you about your order (for example, if an item is unavailable);
  • issue and redeem promotional codes;
  • respond to your feedback, questions, and support requests;
  • maintain the security, integrity, and performance of the Service;
  • comply with legal obligations, including tax and food-service record-keeping.

3. How we share information

  • We do not sell your personal information. We do not rent or trade personal information to third parties for their marketing.
  • Service providers: we share order information with the service providers needed to run the Service — including payment processors (including Clover) and hosting/delivery providers — only to the extent necessary for them to perform their services.
  • Reviews: if you choose to post a review on a third-party platform (such as Google), that review is governed by that platform's privacy policy.
  • Legal requirements: we may disclose information if required by law, court order, or to protect the rights, safety, and property of SHAWA, our guests, or others.

4. Data retention

Order records are retained for as long as needed for accounting, tax, and legal purposes and for resolving disputes. On-device data (cart, preferences) remains on your device until you clear it. Feedback and review records are retained while the related promotion or business record is active.
